# Focus Scroll — Privacy Policy

This repository hosts the privacy policy for **Focus Scroll**, a
distraction-free article reader extension for Chrome with a scroll-driven
focus zone, highlights, and notes.

📄 **Read the policy:** https://sandeep-hegde.github.io/focus-scroll-privacy/

## The short version

- Everything stays on your device — highlights, notes, and preferences
- No tracking, no analytics, no accounts, no ads, no servers
- The extension only runs when you activate it
- You can export or delete your data at any time

The policy page has the full details, including the one honest nuance
(reader *preferences* travel through Chrome's own sync if you have it
enabled — your highlights and notes never do).

## Updates

The policy is versioned with this repository. If a future version of
Focus Scroll changes what is stored or where, the policy is updated here
**before** that version ships — the commit history is the changelog.
